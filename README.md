# Focusel

A voice assistant that lives in your MacBook's notch. Press a shortcut, speak, and Focusel searches, sets timers and works with Notes, Calendar, Gmail, Notion, GitHub and more. Your personal data stays on your Mac.

**[Download for macOS](https://github.com/KNMNikhil/Focusel/releases/latest/download/Focusel.dmg)** · [Website](https://focusel.space) · [All releases](https://github.com/KNMNikhil/Focusel/releases)

## Install

1. Download `Focusel.dmg` and drag Focusel into Applications.
2. The first time, macOS may say it can't open the app. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Follow the short setup. Focusel updates itself after that (Settings → About).

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon running a recent macOS.
